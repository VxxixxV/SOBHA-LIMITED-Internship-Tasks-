# SOBHA LIMITED Internship Tasks

During my 3-month internship as a **Data Analyst Intern** in the IT Department at SOBHA Limited, I worked extensively with large-scale business datasets and contributed to multiple data-driven projects. My responsibilities included **Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, Wireframing, and Data Visualization** using **Microsoft Excel** and primarily **Power BI**.

I designed and developed several **interactive business-level dashboards** to support data analysis and decision-making processes. These dashboards were reviewed and verified by my mentor and senior professionals within the organization. Through this internship, I gained hands-on experience in transforming raw data into meaningful insights, improving reporting efficiency, and presenting data in a visually impactful manner.

### Skills & Tools Used

* Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* Data Modeling
* Dashboard Development
* Wireframing & Report Design
* Data Visualization
* Microsoft Excel
* Power BI

### Key Highlights

* Worked with large and complex datasets
* Built interactive and dynamic dashboards
* Improved data interpretation and reporting workflows
* Collaborated with mentors and senior team members for dashboard validation and enhancements
* Applied analytical thinking to solve real-world business problems
